# Reel pipeline (with approval gate)

## 1. Research
- Search the topic, collect 5–8 facts with sources, and note what is new or surprising.

## 2. Questions for the creator
- Ask at most 5 questions only the creator can answer (their story, opinion, numbers).

## 3. Proposal
- Give 3 hook options, the angle, the length (30–60 s) and the format (talking head, split screen, screen proof).

## 4. Script
- Hook in the first 2 seconds, one idea, proof on screen, one clear CTA.
- Mark each line: [A-roll] / [screen] / [graphic].

## 5. Approval gate
- STOP. Show the script and wait for the word "approved". Change nothing after approval without asking.

## 6. Edit (Claude Code)
- Cut the A-roll on sentence boundaries (never mid-word).
- Captions with one highlighted word per line.
- Graphics never cover the face.
- Audio: voice −16 LUFS, music −27 LUFS ducked, master −14 LUFS.

## 7. QC
- Re-transcribe the export, then check the frame grid, loudness, and that the face is clear in every frame.

## 8. Schedule
- Write the caption (hook within 125 chars, CTA, 4 hashtags).
- Schedule at the creator's best time. Confirm the post time back to the creator.
