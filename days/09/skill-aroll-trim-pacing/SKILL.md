# A-roll trim & pacing

## Inputs
A transcript with timestamps (word-level if possible) and the raw clip.

## Rules
1. **The story comes first.** Find the one-sentence promise. The first 3 seconds must state or show it.
2. **Best take wins.** When a line is repeated, keep the last complete, confident take unless an earlier one has better energy.
3. **Cut on silences, never mid-word.** Leave 80–120 ms of air before and after each line.
4. **Kill false starts, filler words and "so, um" openers.** Keep a laugh or a reaction if it adds personality.
5. **One idea per 3–6 seconds.** Use a visual change (punch-in, B-roll, graphic) at least every 4 seconds.
6. **Never end mid-sentence.** The last word must land, then cut within 0.3 s.
7. **Payoff before the ask.** Deliver the value, then the CTA.

## Output
A cut list (`in`, `out`, line text, reason), plus suggested B-roll or graphic moments with timestamps.
