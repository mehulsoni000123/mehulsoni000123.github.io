# Expert outreach for a video

## 1. Read the brief
- Read the script or outline. List the 5–10 questions an expert must answer.
- Ask the user: how many people, which countries/languages, and the deadline.

## 2. Find people (web search)
- Look for: university pages, published papers, institutes, journalists who cover the topic.
- For each person, record name, role, institution, why they fit (one line, citing their work), and a PUBLIC work email from an official page.
- No email found → skip them. Never guess or construct addresses.
- Save to `outreach.csv`.

## 3. Draft emails
- Subject: specific, under 8 words (e.g. "Quick question on your Mekong delta research").
- Body under 120 words:
  1. who I am (one line)
  2. why them (cite their work)
  3. the ask: a 20-minute call or 3 written answers
  4. what they get (credit in the video, a link)
  5. an easy out ("no worries if not")
- Optionally attach 1–2 screenshots of the concept.
- Show the user 3 drafts first.

## 4. Send only with approval
- Send in batches of 10, from the user's own account, after the user says "send".
- Log the sent time in the CSV.

## 5. Track replies
- Each day, check the inbox for replies. Update replied / call booked / declined.
- Draft the replies for the user to approve.
- Follow up once after 7 days if there was no reply; never more.
